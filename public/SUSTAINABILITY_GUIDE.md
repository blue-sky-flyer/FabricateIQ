# Sustainability Guide for Trade Show Booth Fabrication

Adapted from Mosaic's "Production Guide to Sustainable Events and Activations" — focused on booth-specific material and service alternatives relevant to quote generation.

## How to Use This Guide

This guide provides sustainability vectors (recommended alternatives) but **does NOT include specific pricing**. When generating sustainability enhancements for a quote:

1. Compare each material/component line item against the alternatives below
2. Estimate cost deltas using MASTER_CATALOG reference prices + the estimation guidelines at the bottom
3. Mark all cost estimates with appropriate confidence levels
4. Focus on material substitutions that are commercially available today

## 1. Fabrication & Builds

### Wood & Structural Materials

| Standard Material | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Standard MDF | FSC/PEPFC certified wood or 50%+ recycled MDF with reduced formaldehyde emissions | HIGH | FSC certification ensures responsible forest management |
| Standard acrylic paint | Plant-derived acrylic paint | MEDIUM | Reduces petroleum-based chemical use |
| Standard oak/hardwood | Bamboo (structural or decorative) | HIGH | Bamboo grows up to 3 ft/day, requires far less water than hardwood, compostable waste |
| Steel/concrete structural | Mass timber (CLT, LSL, LVL, NLT, GLT) | HIGH | Significantly lower carbon footprint than steel/concrete; gaining traction as a substitute |
| Custom one-off builds | Modular/reusable booth systems (AGAM, Octanorm) | HIGH | Design for year-over-year reuse with quality materials; higher upfront but amortizes over multiple events |
| Single-use custom millwork | Design-for-reuse millwork | HIGH | Work with clients to develop multi-event plans; invest in durable materials for longevity |

### Flooring

| Standard Material | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Single-use carpet | Reusable carpet tiles or modular flooring | HIGH | Can be reused across multiple events; significantly reduces waste |
| New G-Floor | G-Floor reprint (reuse existing base) | HIGH | Reprinting existing G-Floor saves ~60% vs manufacturing new |
| Standard vinyl flooring | Recycled-content vinyl or natural fiber flooring | MEDIUM | Look for products with recycled content certifications |
| Astro turf (single-use) | Reusable artificial turf panels | MEDIUM | Modular panels can be stored and reused |

### Furniture

| Standard Material | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| New custom furniture fabrication | Furniture rental from local suppliers | HIGH | Rental saves 40-60% vs new fabrication and eliminates post-event waste |
| Standard wood furniture | Reclaimed or FSC-certified wood furniture | MEDIUM | Reduces demand for virgin timber |
| Standard MDF pedestals/risers | Bamboo or reclaimed wood alternatives | MEDIUM | Bamboo is renewable; reclaimed wood reduces waste |

## 2. Branding, Signage & Wayfinding

| Standard Material | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| PVC banners | SEG recyclable fabric graphics | HIGH | SEG fabric is already recyclable; PVC is not biodegradable |
| Solvent-based printing inks | Water-based / biodegradable inks | MEDIUM | No petroleum, fewer harsh chemicals; not 100% renewable but significantly better |
| Event-specific printed signage | Generic/reusable signage (no dates or event-specific branding) | HIGH | Can be reused across 3+ events; 30-50% savings over time |
| Printed banners for sponsor logos | Digital screens or projected branding | HIGH | Eliminates print waste entirely; screens can rotate multiple sponsors |
| Vinyl wrap graphics | Recyclable fabric wrap or water-based vinyl | MEDIUM | Standard vinyl is difficult to recycle |
| Foamcore inserts | Recycled-content foamcore or cardboard alternatives | LOW | Marginal improvement but better end-of-life options |

## 3. AV & Energy

| Standard Material | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Halogen/incandescent lighting | LED lighting | HIGH | Most energy-efficient option; similar or lower cost; longer lifespan |
| Diesel generators (temporary power) | Battery-powered generators or solar panels | HIGH | Eliminates diesel emissions; solar panels viable for outdoor events |
| Standard AV equipment from distant supplier | Cross-rent AV locally | MEDIUM | Reduces shipping emissions; supports local economy |
| High-power display equipment | Low-power/energy-efficient displays | MEDIUM | Choose equipment with best energy ratings |

## 4. Transportation & Logistics

| Standard Approach | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Standard freight shipping | Local sourcing (suppliers within 50km) | MEDIUM | Reduces transport emissions 5-10%; supports local economy |
| Multiple delivery trips | Efficient vehicle packing / consolidated shipments | MEDIUM | Reduces trips and fuel consumption |
| Standard delivery vehicles | EV or hybrid transport vehicles | HIGH | Significant emissions reduction; increasingly available |
| Off-site storage requiring transport | Venue storage (if available) | LOW | Eliminates return transport; depends on venue capacity |

## 5. Waste Reduction & Operations

| Standard Approach | Sustainable Alternative | Environmental Impact | Notes |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Single-use build materials | Design for disassembly and reuse | HIGH | Plan builds that can be broken down and stored for future events |
| Dispose of leftover materials | Donate to schools, art programs, or recycling | MEDIUM | Reduces landfill; community benefit |
| No waste tracking | Waste diversion plan with measurement | LOW | Enables benchmarking and improvement over time |
| Standard packaging | Reusable crates and minimal packaging | MEDIUM | Reduces packaging waste across multiple events |

## 6. Carbon Offsets (Last Resort)

Carbon offsets should be considered only AFTER reducing and reusing. They are the last step in making an event more sustainable.

| Program Type | Cost Estimate | Confidence | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|------------|-------|
| Verified carbon offsets (UNFCCC, VCS, Carbon Trust) | $15-50 per tonne CO2e | MEDIUM | Should be additional, verified, and permanent |
| Reforestation programs | $10-30 per tonne CO2e | LOW | Risk of reversal; long-term commitment needed |
| Renewable energy credits | $5-20 per tonne CO2e | MEDIUM | Support wind, solar, hydropower projects |

**Typical booth carbon footprint:** 2-10 tonnes CO2e depending on size, materials, and logistics distance.

## Cost Estimation Guidelines

When estimating the cost of sustainable alternatives, use these rules of thumb:

### Material Premiums (vs standard)
| Alternative | Typical Premium | Confidence |
|-------------|----------------|------------|
| FSC-certified wood vs standard MDF | +10-20% | MEDIUM |
| Bamboo vs standard hardwood | +0-10% (similar to oak pricing) | LOW |
| Plant-derived acrylic paint | +15-25% | LOW |
| Recycled-content MDF | +5-15% | MEDIUM |
| Mass timber vs steel framing | -10-20% (labour savings offset material) | LOW |

### Savings from Reuse/Rental
| Alternative | Typical Savings | Confidence |
|-------------|----------------|------------|
| Furniture rental vs new fabrication | -40-60% | HIGH |
| G-Floor reprint vs new G-Floor | -60% | HIGH (catalog-backed: $10.86 vs $27.27/sqft) |
| Reusable carpet vs single-use | -30-50% over 3 events | MEDIUM |
| Reusable signage vs event-specific | -30-50% over 3 events | MEDIUM |
| Modular booth system vs custom one-off | +15% upfront, -40% over 3 events | MEDIUM |

### Logistics Savings
| Alternative | Typical Savings | Confidence |
|-------------|----------------|------------|
| Local sourcing vs distant suppliers | -5-10% on logistics line items | MEDIUM |
| Consolidated shipping vs multiple trips | -15-25% on logistics | LOW |
| EV/hybrid transport | +5-10% (current premium) | LOW |

### Signage & Graphics
| Alternative | Typical Premium/Savings | Confidence |
|-------------|------------------------|------------|
| SEG fabric vs PVC banners | +0-10% (comparable pricing) | HIGH (catalog-backed: $34.04/sqft) |
| Water-based inks vs solvent | +5-15% | LOW |
| Digital screens vs printed banners | Variable — depends on rental vs purchase | MEDIUM |
| Recyclable fabric wrap vs vinyl wrap | +10-20% | LOW |

### How to Apply
1. For each material line item in the quote, find the closest match in the tables above
2. Apply the premium/savings percentage to the original line item cost
3. Round to whole dollars
4. Set confidence based on the table (HIGH if catalog-backed, MEDIUM if industry knowledge, LOW if speculative)
5. If no suitable alternative exists for a line item, skip it — not every item needs a sustainability suggestion
6. For "operations" category items (waste plans, carbon offsets), estimate based on project size:
   - Small booth (<200 sqft): $200-500 for offset program
   - Medium booth (200-600 sqft): $500-1,500
   - Large booth (600+ sqft): $1,500-5,000
